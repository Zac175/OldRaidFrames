OldRaidFrames - Changelog
=========================

v1.1.1 (2026-11-25)
- The addon has a website for bug reports and feature suggestions:
  /orf report and /orf suggest give you a link with your version and
  game filled in, and /orf site opens the list of what changed.
- Report and Suggest buttons in the options window do the same.

v1.1.0 (2026-11-22)
- WoW Forever and Retail: the addon now loads and works there as well
  as on Classic Era and Burning Crusade Anniversary - one folder, the
  same files. Those clients hide some values from addons during a
  fight; the README's "WoW Forever and Retail" section lists what goes
  quiet until the value can be read again.
- Party frames: the same frames for a 5-player group, under a gold
  "Party" title where Group 1 stands in a raid. Display Party Frames
  and Show Yourself in Party in /orf (or /orf party, /orf partyplayer).
- Fixed on WoW Forever: an error from the heal prediction library when
  it loaded before the game's own aura reader existed.
